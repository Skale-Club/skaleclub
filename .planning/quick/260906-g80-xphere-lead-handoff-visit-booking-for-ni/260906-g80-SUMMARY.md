# Quick Task 260906-g80: Xphere Lead Handoff + Visit Booking Summary

**Completed barbershop leads now flow into Xphere through an idempotent outbox (retry/dead_letter/reconcile) and are redirected to a prefilled https://xphere.app/book page picked from their in-person/online answer, all configured from a new admin card with no hardcoded tenant identifiers.**

## Accomplishments

- Ported the skaleclub-websites Xphere module to this single-tenant repo: `integration_deliveries` outbox, `Idempotency-Key == event_id`, backoff `[1m, 5m, 30m, 2h, 8h, 24h]`, dead_letter after permanent HTTP errors (400/401/403/409/413/422) or 8 attempts, stale-lock release, reconcile of missed completed leads.
- Lead submission is never blocked by Xphere: step 4 of `runLeadPostProcessing` is wrapped, and the GHL step is byte-identical (only the header comment, the result type, the new `formSlug` param, and the appended step changed).

- Barbershop form asks "What's your shop address?" only when `tipoVisita = presencial`, in pt-BR on `/barbershops-br` ("Qual é o endereço da sua barbearia?").
