# Editorial service images — 2026-09

Eight service-card covers generated with Codex's built-in image generation tool. Final assets are photorealistic 16:10 WebPs at 1600 x 1000. They intentionally contain no readable copy, logos, trademarks, or watermarks.

## Shared prompt contract

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: 16:10 service-card cover for a premium digital agency website
Style/medium: photorealistic editorial photography, believable natural materials, refined but not staged stock photography
Composition/framing: landscape 16:10, one clear focal point inside the central 80%, safe crop margins, readable at 280px wide
Lighting/mood: cinematic soft light, deep navy and charcoal shadows, subtle electric-blue accents, slightly brighter exposure to survive a dark UI overlay
Constraints: realistic anatomy and hands; plausible equipment; no readable text; no logos; no trademarks; no watermark
Avoid: 3D illustration, isometric art, floating icons, holograms, neon overload, generic corporate smiles, fake gibberish UI, clutter
```

## Per-service prompt subjects

- **Digital Marketing Consultation:** a candid strategy session between a consultant and a small-business owner, collaborating over a laptop and printed analytics at a believable office table.
- **Website Design & Development:** an over-the-shoulder web designer/developer actively building a responsive site at a dual-monitor desk, with plausible abstract layouts and no legible words.
- **Paid Advertising:** a close editorial campaign-review workspace with laptop, phone, printed ad-creative photographs, abstract performance charts, and natural hands comparing variations.
- **Content Creation:** a creator filming a handmade product in a compact real studio with mirrorless camera, tripod, soft light, microphone, and product table.
- **Branding & Graphic Design:** an elegant top-down flat lay of unbranded packaging, paper proofs, business-card shapes, color swatches, embossed finishes, and abstract typographic forms.
- **Lead Generation:** a candid middle-aged Black small-business owner responding to a customer inquiry by phone while reviewing a laptop at a compact workshop showroom front desk.
- **CRM and Marketing Automation:** a no-person operations desk with monitor, tablet, and phone showing abstract pipeline cards, calendar blocks, and message states across realistic physical screens.
- **3D Printing:** a macro three-quarter photograph of a real FDM printer laying down blue filament, with a visible spool, layer texture, and a few useful finished parts.
